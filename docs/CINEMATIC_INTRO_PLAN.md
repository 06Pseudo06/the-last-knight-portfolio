# The Last Knight — Cinematic Intro Implementation Plan

## 1. Cinematic Vision & Concept

The narrative opening of **The Last Knight** sets the tone for the entire portfolio:
1. **The Awakening in the Darkness:** The screen starts in deep obsidian darkness with the sound and faint visual drifting of storm mist and ocean spray.
2. **The Code-Eye Visor:** Inside the horizontal slit of the knight's engraved steel barbute helm, glowing glyphs of code ignite. These words and symbols arrange themselves into a living, generative eye that blinks with human-like breathing and awareness.
3. **The Gaze of Determination:** The eye's pupil dilates, focusing directly on the viewer.
4. **The Cinematic Pullback Transition:** As the eye completes an intentional blink, the camera zooms rapidly backward through the visor aperture, pulling out to reveal the full figure of the Knight standing tall on an open ocean rock amidst heavy clouds, wind, and stormy waves, while a warm golden-crimson ray of dawn light breaks through the clouds.
5. **The Editorial Reveal:** The hero typography ("FORGED THROUGH CURIOSITY.") emerges with staggered letter reveals, and the interface becomes fully interactive.

## 2. Technical Analysis of the Eye Reference (`eye_python`)

The reference project [mishratanya2311/eye_python](https://github.com/mishratanya2311/eye_python) demonstrates how words can generate biological form.

### 2.1 Key Reference Mechanics
1. **Parametric Eyelid Anatomy:**
   - Upper and lower eyelid curves are generated by mathematical functions parameterized across horizontal position $u \in [-1, 1]$:
     $$y_{upper}(u) = -h \cdot \text{env}(u) \cdot (1.0 - 0.11 u - 0.06 u^2) + \text{corner\_line}(u)$$
     $$y_{lower}(u) = +h \cdot \text{env}(u) \cdot (1.0 + 0.13 u - 0.05 u^2) + \text{corner\_line}(u)$$
     $$\text{env}(u) = (1 - u^2)^{0.76}$$
   - Canthal corners incorporate an asymmetric medial dip and lateral sweep for anatomical realism.
2. **Streamline Text Deformation:**
   - Words are placed along dynamic streamline vectors that bend smoothly around the eyeball contour rather than resting on rigid straight lines.
3. **Concentric & Radial Iris Structures:**
   - Multiple concentric rings of words rotated tangentially along circular arcs.
   - Radial spoke typography projecting from the collarette outward to the limbal ring.
4. **Dynamic Blink Motion:**
   - Eyelid curves interpolate over time between open state ($openness = 1.0$) and a downward sleeping arc ($openness = 0.0$), with a tight seam of luminous words appearing along the closed eyelid crease.

### 2.2 Translation from Python to Next.js / WebGL / Canvas
- In Python/Pygame, each frame is drawn into PIL/Pygame software surfaces, which can be computationally intensive at 60fps.
- For the Next.js web application:
  - We translate this mathematical model into an **HTML5 2D Offscreen Canvas / WebGL Instanced Mesh**.
  - Words and code tokens (`async`, `await`, `interface`, `matrix`, `kernel`, `resilience`, `forge`, `knight`) are baked into a crisp text sprite texture atlas once during initialization.
  - The iris, sclera, and eyelid geometry are calculated in TypeScript and rendered with hardware acceleration using WebGL or high-performance 2D Canvas context with pre-computed transform matrices.
  - This guarantees 60fps performance across modern desktop and mobile browsers with zero dropped frames.

## 3. Scene Transition & Choreography

```
Timeline (Total ~4.2 seconds, with instant Skip option):

[0.0s] ───────── [0.8s] ────────── [2.0s] ─────────── [3.2s] ────────── [4.2s]
Deep Black       Visor Slit        Eye Blinks        Camera Zooms Out     Hero UI
Mist Drifts      Glyphs Spark      & Looks           Through Visor Slit   Fades In
Audio/Ambiance   (Obsidian/Gold)   (Cyan/Crimson)    Knight Revealed      Interactive
```

### Phase Details:
- **Phase 1: Darkness & Atmosphere (0.0s – 0.8s)**
  - Fullscreen obsidian canvas (`#090A0C`).
  - Subtle drifting mist and wind particles appear.
  - Audio cue (optional ambient ocean storm, strictly muted by default with explicit sound toggle).
- **Phase 2: Visor Slit Aperture (0.8s – 2.0s)**
  - An SVG/Canvas clipping mask matching the angular barbute helm visor slit expands slightly.
  - Inside the aperture, typography glyphs and code tokens ignite in subtle crimson-golden ember tones.
  - The ocular geometry resolves: concentric code rings form the iris, glowing pupil in the center.
- **Phase 3: The Conscious Blink (2.0s – 3.0s)**
  - The eye tracks the cursor position subtly (or drifts smoothly if touch-based).
  - The eyelid curves execute a fluid blink ($1.0 \to 0.0 \to 1.0$), with eyelid seam glyphs aligning.
- **Phase 4: Cinematic Zoom-Out (3.0s – 3.8s)**
  - GSAP timeline scales the visor canvas outward while fading in the full knight silhouette.
  - Parallax motion separates the knight from the background storm clouds and ocean swells.
- **Phase 5: Editorial Presentation (3.8s – 4.2s)**
  - Navigation header, "FORGED THROUGH CURIOSITY." typography, chapter rail, and footer elements reveal sequentially using staggered `fade-up` easing.
  - Smooth scrolling becomes active.

## 4. Performance & Device Strategy

1. **Device Tier Detection:**
   - Detect device pixel ratio (DPR) and GPU concurrency.
   - On low-power mobile devices or low FPS detection:
     - Cap canvas DPR at 1.0.
     - Reduce typography ring density.
     - Simplify background shader passes.
2. **Skip Intro Mechanism:**
   - A persistent, high-contrast `"SKIP INTRO [ESC]"` button in the top corner allows immediate transition to the completed Hero state.
   - Intro playback status is recorded in `sessionStorage` so returning visitors to the home route are not forced to re-watch the sequence.
3. **Accessibility (`prefers-reduced-motion`):**
   - If reduced motion is preferred by the operating system, the intro sequence is bypassed completely. The hero loads directly in its final, stationary state with instant visibility.
